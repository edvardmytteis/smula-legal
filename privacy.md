# Smula Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Smula helps you make dinner from what you already have at home. We built it to know as little about you as
possible: you don't create an account, and what you enter stays on your phone.

**Data controller:** Edvard Mytteis. Contact: [edvardmytteis@gmail.com](mailto:edvardmytteis@gmail.com).

## What stays on your phone

Allergies, diet, dislikes, what you always have at home, number of servings and the dinners you have made are
stored on your phone. If iCloud is on, they sync to your own iCloud so they follow you to a new phone. We have
no access to them.

## What is sent when you ask for dinner

To make suggestions and recipes, the app sends to our server:

- the groceries you want to use up and what you always have at home
- allergies, diet and dislikes
- time, servings and whether you can shop
- country and language
- the titles of your recent dinners and what you liked, so suggestions vary
- when you take a photo of your groceries: the photo

Our server passes this to OpenAI, which writes the suggestions and reads the photo. We do not store the content
of the request, and the photo is deleted as soon as it has been read. We ask OpenAI not to store the request.
OpenAI may still keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse of its service, after which it is deleted.

## What we store on our server

- **An anonymous user ID**, created the first time you open the app. It is not linked to your name, email or
  phone number.
- **Usage:** the type of request (suggestions, recipe or photo), when it was made and what it cost us. We use
  this to keep quotas and costs under control. The content is not stored.
- **Subscription status:** whether you subscribe, and when it ends.
- **A security key from Apple (App Attest)**, which shows that requests come from the genuine app on a real
  device. It says nothing about who you are.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat keeps track of the
subscription for us, linked to the anonymous user ID.

## Who processes data for us

| Provider | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Server and database | EU (Stockholm) |
| OpenAI | Writes suggestions and recipes, reads photos | USA |
| RevenueCat | Subscription status | USA |
| Apple | Purchases, App Attest, iCloud | Under Apple's terms |

Transfers to the USA rely on the EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

## Legal basis

We process this information to provide the service you ask for (contract), and to prevent abuse and keep costs
down (legitimate interest). Allergies and diet can say something about your health. They are only used to make
safe recipes for you, and are not stored by us.

## How long

Usage records are deleted after 12 months. The anonymous user ID and subscription status are kept while you use
the app. If you delete your data (see below), everything we hold on our server is deleted.

## Your rights

You can ask for access, correction and deletion, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in
Norway, Datatilsynet). Data on your phone is deleted when you delete the app. To delete what we hold on our
server, use "Delete my data" in the app's Settings, or write to
[edvardmytteis@gmail.com](mailto:edvardmytteis@gmail.com).

## Children

Smula is not made for children under 13.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and tell you about significant changes in the app.
